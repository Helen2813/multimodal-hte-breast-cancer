In the locked primary analysis of 559 HR-positive/HER2-negative day-180 landmark survivors, early hormone-therapy initiation was associated with a 23.0-day treated-minus-control difference in 730-day overlap-population RMST (95% patient-bootstrap percentile interval, -4.2 to 91.0 days). The interval included zero, although 95.7% of 300 bootstrap repetitions were positive. All 6,000 nuisance-partition fits completed, and inner cross-fitting Monte Carlo error was small relative to sampling uncertainty.
