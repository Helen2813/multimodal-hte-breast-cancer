\subsection{Conclusion}

After protocol locking and full patient-level bootstrap inference, early hormone-therapy initiation was associated with a 22.95-day positive ATO contrast in 730-day post-landmark RMST. The 95\% percentile bootstrap interval (-4.17 to 91.01 days) included zero. The analysis therefore supports a stable positive direction but not a definitive treatment-benefit claim. More broadly, the study shows that repeated cross-fitting can control nuisance-partition randomness while leaving clinically important sampling and design uncertainty visible rather than concealing it.
